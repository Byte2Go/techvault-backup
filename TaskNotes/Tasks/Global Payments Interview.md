R1 Interview Invite || Solution Architect || Mayank Tiwari

**Specialties**

Payments, Ecommerce, Point of sale, Payment technology, Merchant services, Integrated payments, Software solutions, Omnichannel payments, Cross-border payments, Financial services, Mobile payments, Digital wallets, Online payments, Unified commerce, Contactless payments, Embedded fintech, Issuing, Mobile point of sale, Fintech, Issuer solutions, and B2B payments

**Job Summary**

We are seeking an experienced Solution Architect to design and govern solutions for merchant acquiring and payment acceptance platforms. The role focuses on merchant onboarding, transaction processing, routing, clearing, settlement, reconciliation, and reporting, supporting high-volume, low-latency card payment flows.

The ideal candidate will have strong exposure to acquiring processors, payment gateways, and merchant ecosystems, with experience in architecting secure, scalable, and highly available platforms in a global 24x7 payments environment.

**Key Responsibilities**:

**Merchant Acquiring Architecture**

• Design end-to-end architectures for merchant payment acceptance platforms including POS, e-commerce, and omnichannel flows

• Architect authorization routing, clearing, settlement, and merchant funding solutions

• Design for high TPS, peak traffic handling, and strict SLAs

**Gateway & Integration Design**

• Architect payment gateway APIs, SDKs, and webhooks

• Design integrations with card networks (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover), acquirers, banks, and merchant systems

• Define API standards, idempotency, retries, and error handling

**Security, Compliance & Risk**

• Ensure PCI-DSS and card scheme compliance

• Design secure flows using tokenization, encryption, HSMs, and vaulting

• Embed fraud monitoring, auditability, and observability

**High Availability & Resilience**

• Design active-active, multi-region deployments

• Define DR strategies, failover mechanisms, and operational readiness

• Ensure platform resilience during traffic spikes and failures

**Legacy & Modern Platform Coexistence**

• Architect solutions across legacy acquiring platforms and modern cloud-native systems

• Drive API enablement and event-driven modernization

• Ensure backward compatibility during migrations

**Delivery & Architecture Governance**

• Act as technical authority for acquiring initiatives

• Review designs, APIs, data models, and infrastructure

• Identify and mitigate performance, security, and compliance risks

**Stakeholder Collaboration**

• Partner with product, engineering, compliance, operations, and client teams

• Support merchant onboarding, partner integrations, and solutioning discussions

• Communicate architectural decisions to senior stakeholders

**Required Skills & Experience**

• 20+ years of IT experience with 10+ years in Solution Architecture

• Strong experience in merchant acquiring or payment gateway platforms

• Deep understanding of authorization, settlement, reconciliation, and funding

• Experience with REST APIs, event-driven systems, Kafka/MQ, RDBMS & NoSQL

• Cloud experience with AWS, Azure, or GCP

• Strong communication and leadership skills

**Preferred / Nice to Have**

• Exposure to scheme certifications and mandates

• Knowledge of fraud, chargebacks, and dispute systems

• Architecture certifications (AWS, GCP,TOGAF, Azure)
