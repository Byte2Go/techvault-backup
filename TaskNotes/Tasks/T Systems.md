JD - Solution Designer - T Systems
----

**Role - Solution Designer**

**Loc - Pune** 

We are seeking a detail oriented and technically proficient Solution Designer to translate business requirements into functional technology solutions  The ideal candidate will collaborate with stakeholders  architects  and development teams to design scalable  robust  and secure systems that meet the organization's strategic goals.  
  
  
**Key Responsibilities** 
Analyze business needs and translate them into detailed system designs and technical specifications  
Design end to end solutions that align with enterprise architecture  business strategy  and user needs  
Collaborate with enterprise and solution architects to ensure alignment with architectural standards  
Create solution design documents  data flow diagrams  and system integration designs  
Ensure solutions are technically feasible  cost effective  and delivered on time  
Work closely with development  QA  and operations teams to support solution implementation  
Identify risks and propose mitigation strategies during the design phase  
Assist in evaluating new technologies and tools to improve system performance and capabilities  
Provide input into project estimates  planning  and technical evaluations  
Ensure that non functional requirements  performance  scalability  security  are addressed in the solution design
